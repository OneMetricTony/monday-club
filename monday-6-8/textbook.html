🌊 Waves — the essentials

Watch each animation, read the definition, then test yourself in the section quizzes (every question has a 🤖 Claude help button).

1 · Travelling waves

A travelling wave carries energy (and the disturbance) along — but the medium itself only oscillates in place (watch the red dot).

Wave basics

Amplitude = max displacement from rest. Wavelength (λ) = distance between two points in phase (e.g. crest to crest). Frequency (f) = oscillations per second (Hz). Period (T) = time for one cycle, T = 1/f.

Wave equation

Wave speed v = f λ. Transverse waves oscillate perpendicular to travel (light, water surface); longitudinal waves oscillate along the travel direction (sound).

C3 — Wave phenomena

Reflection (bounces back), refraction (bends when speed changes, Snell's law n₁sinθ₁ = n₂sinθ₂), diffraction (spreads through gaps/around edges, strongest when gap ≈ λ), and interference (waves superpose: in phase → constructive, antiphase → destructive).

2 · Standing waves

Two identical waves travelling opposite ways superpose into a standing wave. Yellow dots = nodes (never move); halfway between = antinodes (max swing).

C4 — Standing waves

Formed by superposition of two waves of equal frequency/amplitude moving in opposite directions. Nodes have zero amplitude; antinodes have maximum. They don't transfer energy along the medium. Boundary conditions fix which wavelengths "fit" → the harmonics.

Harmonics

String / pipe open at both ends: fₙ = n·v/2L. Pipe closed at one end (only odd harmonics): fₙ = n·v/4L, n = 1,3,5… The lowest is the fundamental (first harmonic).

3 · The Doppler effect

A moving source (red) crowds its wavefronts ahead of it (higher frequency / pitch) and stretches them behind (lower) — that's the Doppler effect.

C5 — Doppler effect

The observed frequency changes when source and observer move relative to each other. Approaching → higher observed frequency; receding → lower. For sound: f′ = f · (v ± v_o)/(v ∓ v_s). For light, a receding source is red-shifted, approaching is blue-shifted.
