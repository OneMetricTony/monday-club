1A shirt costs $40. It is first discounted 25%, then 10% tax is added to the discounted price. What is the final price?▸ solution
Discount: 40 × 0.75 = $30. Tax: 30 × 1.10 = $33.
2The average (mean) of five numbers is 18. Four of the numbers are 12, 20, 15, and 25. What is the fifth number?▸ solution
Total = 5 × 18 = 90. Known four sum to 72. Fifth = 90 − 72 = 18.
3A rectangle has perimeter 36 and its length is twice its width. What is its area?▸ solution
l + w = 18 and l = 2w ⟹ 3w = 18 ⟹ w = 6, l = 12. Area = 12 × 6 = 72.
4How many whole numbers from 1 to 100 are divisible by 3 or by 5?▸ solution
Div by 3: 33. Div by 5: 20. Div by 15 (both): 6. By inclusion–exclusion: 33 + 20 − 6 = 47.
