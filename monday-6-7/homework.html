1 · Arithmetic grid

✅ Show solution

Take the top-left entry as 1, the horizontal step as a and the vertical step as b. The entry in row i, column j (counting from 0) is 1 + j·a + i·b. The bottom-right entry 1 + 7a + 7b < 75 forces a + b ≤ 10. Column 5 is j = 4, so its entries are 1 + 4a + i·b; setting one equal to 45 gives 4a + i·b = 44 with 0 ≤ i ≤ 7. Checking each a leaves the grids (a,b) = (1,8), (2,6), (4,4), (5,4), (6,4), (8,2) — that's 6 grids, so the answer is (A) 6.
